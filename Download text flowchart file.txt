KEY FEEDBACK LOOPS
==================

1. DATA QUALITY LOOP
--------------------
Invalid Data / Unclassified Customer
  |
  v
Correct / Clean / Reclassify
  |
  v
Reprocess Customer 360
  |
  v
Validate Again
  |
  v
Coverage = 100%

Business Purpose:
Ensures no customer is missed and avoids wrong outreach.


2. RE-ENGAGEMENT LOOP
---------------------
Customer Does Not Book Service
  |
  v
Retry Strategy
(Change offer, channel, timing, or advisor follow-up)
  |
  v
Back to Best Treatment Journey
  |
  v
Execute Outreach Again

Business Purpose:
Prevents losing customers after one failed attempt.


3. DEALER ROOT CAUSE LOOP
-------------------------
Dealer Process Issue Detected
(Repeat repair, delay, poor communication, complaint, bad service experience)
  |
  v
Create Dealer Corrective Action
  |
  v
Inspection Checklist / Repair Validation / Advisor Training / Escalation
  |
  v
Monitor CSI, NPS, Repeat Repair, Complaint Resolution
  |
  v
Improve Dealer Service Quality

Business Purpose:
Fixes the reason customers are leaving, not just the campaign response.


4. MODEL LEARNING LOOP
----------------------
Campaign Outcome Captured
  |
  v
Measure ROI, Retention, Revenue Recovery, Churn Reduction
  |
  v
Update Risk Model, NBA Logic, Offer Rules, Channel Timing
  |
  v
Improve Future Recommendations
  |
  v
Back to AI Risk and Opportunity Engine

Business Purpose:
Makes the Revenue Recovery Engine continuously improve over time.


MANAGER-READY SUMMARY
=====================

The Revenue Recovery Engine works as a closed-loop decision system. It starts
with complete customer coverage, validates customer and vehicle data, assigns
each customer to one primary lifecycle segment, calculates risk and revenue
opportunity, selects the best treatment journey, executes the approved outreach
or advisor action, measures business outcomes, and continuously updates the AI
model.

The key strength of the flow is that no customer is missed. Invalid data goes
into a data quality loop, non-responding customers go into a re-engagement loop,
dealer service issues go into a root-cause improvement loop, and all outcomes
feed back into a model learning loop.
